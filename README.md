# App Hub for Android

App Hub shows the apps installed on your phone. By Rednave.

## Install

Requires Android 10 or newer (best on Android 13+).

1. Copy the APK file to your phone (USB, Bluetooth, cloud drive — whatever you like) and tap it.
2. Allow "install from unknown sources" when asked.
3. Open the app from your app drawer.

## Using App Hub

- Browse your installed launchable apps, or filter to Wonderdesk apps only.
- Open an app, jump to its Android details and permissions screen, or request an uninstall (Android always asks first).

App Hub is not an app store: it cannot install or update anything itself. Managed suite updates are planned for the future.

## Look and feel

Open About inside the app to choose between Aqua, Bliss, Dark Bliss and Super, and to adjust motion. Aqua is the default.
